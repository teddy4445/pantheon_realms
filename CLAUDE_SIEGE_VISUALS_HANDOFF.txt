4. BEHAVIOR TO PRESERVE

The background is a whole-view painting, not a tiny center crop stretched across
the battlefield. Do not apply a second vertical camera squash to it.

Wall rows must join into one continuous surface. The current implementation uses
uniform texture scale and a shared world-space repeat phase, while retaining the
existing individual damageable structure IDs. Do not stretch a full wall atlas
independently into every hex. The north wall extension closes the tower join.

The gate opening, approach road, bridge and moat must agree with the actual gate
row, wall column and moat cells. The moat uses repeated middle sections with
rounded caps only at its outer ends. The bridge crosses at the real gate.

Gate, towers, walls and keep select intact, damaged or rubble art from their
existing structure state. Destroyed wall sections remain visually breached.
Ground contact and scale should remain stable when switching damage states.

Keep the quieter grid, contact shadows and extra framing space for the keep.
Intact structures expose their labels on hover; damaged labels remain visible.
The existing HUD continues to expose structure status.

This is a presentation and artwork change. Preserve combat rules, targeting,
movement costs, siege levels and save formats. Do not change them to compensate
for an art-placement problem. Do not modify the landing page for this task.
