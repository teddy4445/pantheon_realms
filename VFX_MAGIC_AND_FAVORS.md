# Magic and Favor VFX — what to add later

_Generated by `npm run vfx:list` (tools/assets/vfx/) from the content catalog; the looks are hand-written in `tools/assets/vfx/briefs.ts`. Regenerate after adding a spell, miracle or gift._

**Status: a production list for final art.** Every effect below is drawn today by *code-drawn placeholders* (since 2026-10-03, see below); none has final art yet. This is the list of visual effects to make for magic (spells) and Favor (divine interventions, divine gifts, the Temple offering). Nothing here changes rules: effects are presentation, play after the rules have resolved, and never decide an outcome (instant playback skips them).

## What the game shows today

Code-drawn placeholders (PixiJS shapes and particles, no image files) draw the one-shot phases of every effect on this list (the two map `aura` loops are not drawn yet) from a per-spell *look* — colour school, travel, impact, whole-field moment, reach and sound family — in `packages/presentation/src/fxLooks.ts` (a condensed form of the looks below), painted by `packages/presentation/src/fx.ts`. Before each phase the views look for a strip under that phase’s asset ID in the sprite manifest and play it instead, so a delivery replaces the placeholder with no code change.

- **Battle spells:** the sigil at the commander’s banner corner (`cast`), the bolt, orb, arrow, swarm, wisps or sky strike (`travel`), the impact on each stack the spell reaches — the target, the stacks beside it for area spells, the whole army for army spells (`impact`) — and for army spells a moment over the field (`screen`: night, storm, glow, wind, mist, rain, sun, dusk); then the numbers or the effect’s word.
- **Adventure spells:** the sigil on the hero; a ring spreading over the chosen tile as the fog lifts (Far Sight family, `area`); wind round the hero (Fleet Foot, Wings of Hermes); a column of light lifting the hero away and setting it down at the town (Homecoming, `depart`/`arrive`).
- **Miracles (divine interventions):** the painted miracle card first (when delivered), then in battle the field moment and the light, dome, banner or smite on each stack; on the map, wind, mist or motes on every hero, light over the chosen town, coins over the capital and over the resource bar’s Gold counter.
- **Divine gifts and the Temple offering:** an interface burst rising from the Temple on the town screen — coins, timber and stone, crystals, or light (and light at each dwelling for Blessed Brood). These are CSS placeholders for `fx.gift.*` / `fx.offering.*`.
- **Status effects:** each status leaves a looping overlay on the stack with its own shape (rising chevrons, a ground ring with studs, a broken ring, wind arcs, violet haze, gold sparkles, a grey cloud with a crow, a shield, a dome), held still on Low quality and under Reduce motion (`fx.status.*`).
- **Low quality** (`effects: simple`) keeps a short flash per phase and leaves out the whole-field moments; **Reduce motion** and instant playback skip the effects.

## Conventions for every effect

- **Asset IDs:** `fx.spell.<spellId>.<phase>`, `fx.miracle.<interventionId>.<phase>`, `fx.gift.<giftId>`, `fx.offering.<phase>`, and the shared looping overlays `fx.status.<name>`. Each becomes an entry in `assets-runtime/sprites/manifest.json` (kind `effect`) like today’s `spell.*` placeholders, so a delivery is an art drop + import, never a code change.
- **Phases:** `cast` (on the caster: the commander’s banner corner in battle, the hero on the map), `travel` (a projectile, only for targeted damage), `impact` (on the target, anchored at its feet), `screen` (a full-field overlay for army-wide spells and battle miracles), `area` (a ring on the map for revealing spells), `aura` (a short loop while a map effect lasts), `depart`/`arrive` (Homecoming).
- **Strips:** single horizontal strips, transparent PNG, frames left to right: `cast` 6 frames @ 10 fps; `travel` 4-frame loop @ 12 fps; `impact` 8 frames @ 12 fps; `screen` 12 frames @ 12 fps; status overlays 6-frame loops @ 6 fps. Cell size in hexes: single target 1.6 hex wide (2.2 for two-hex creatures, scaled by the view), area spells 3 hexes, map `area` sized to the spell’s reveal radius.
- **Colour:** a pantheon’s spells and miracles use its accent; shared spells use the school colour — strike white-blue, fire orange, healing green-gold, blessing gold, curse violet-grey, wind white.
- **Accessibility:** no full-screen flashes when Reduce motion is on (the effect plays without the flash frames); status overlays never rely on colour alone (each has its own shape), and the numbers stay in the stack’s statistics.
- **Sound:** every effect pairs with a sound ID from `assets-runtime/audio/manifest.json`: `magic.cast` when a spell gathers, then `magic.<family>` from its look (strike, thunder, fire, heal, bless, curse, wind, frost, nature, swarm, water, shadow, holy, smite, reveal, teleport, coins, horn) — synthesized placeholders listed in the audio pipeline.

## Suggested order

1. The shared status overlays (`fx.status.*`): they make every buff and curse readable on the stacks, and miracles reuse them.
2. The tier-1 battle spells every hero meets first (Spark, Blessing, Ward, War Cry, Swiftness, Ill Omen, Shield of Faith, Hearthlight), then each pantheon’s spells as that pantheon is play-tested.
3. Battle miracles (the “screen” layer pairs with the painted miracle card).
4. Adventure spells and map miracles; then gifts and the offering on the town screen.
