# Pantheon Realms — Art Brief 1 of 3 for GPT-6: STORY ART

**What this file is.** A complete production brief for **92 painted images**:
35 half-body dialogue portraits, 6 campaign covers, 11 ending pictures and 40
mission pictures. Every image has its own description below, its exact size,
and the **exact file name** the game will look for.

**Who reads it.** GPT-6 (the image generator) and the person running it. The
sections "How to work" and "Style bible" apply to every single image. Read
them first, then generate the images one by one, in the order listed.

---

## 0. The game in one paragraph

*Pantheon Realms* is a turn-based fantasy strategy game in the tradition of
classic hero-and-army strategy games. Six mythological pantheons — **Greek,
Norse, Egyptian, Japanese, Celtic and Mesoamerican** — build towns, raise
creatures, lead heroes across an adventure map and fight hex-grid battles for
the favour of their gods. Six story campaigns follow six heroes. The stories
are **original fiction in mythology-inspired settings** (not retellings), told
in short dialogue scenes. The game already has a finished visual identity; the
reference images below define it. **Every image in this brief must look as if
it was painted by the same studio that painted those references.**

---

## 1. How to work (rules for every image)

1. **One image per request**, at the exact pixel size given in its section.
   PNG, sRGB colour.
2. **Name the file exactly** as written (lowercase, dots and underscores, ending
   in `.png`). The game finds every image by this name. Do not add numbers,
   dates or words.
3. **No text of any kind inside the image**: no titles, captions, letters,
   numbers, readable runes, hieroglyphs, kanji or ogham, no logos, signatures
   or watermarks, no speech bubbles, no UI, no frames or borders. Where a story
   needs writing (a scroll, a stela, a rune stone, a talisman), paint
   **abstract decorative marks** that suggest writing without forming real
   letters. The game adds all titles, names and frames itself.
4. **Characters stay the same everywhere.** Section 4 defines the look of all
   35 speaking characters. When a character appears in a cover, an ending or a
   mission picture, use exactly that look (face, hair, skin, costume, colours,
   props).
5. **Adults only, dignified and respectful.** Realistic adult proportions; no
   nudity; battle scenes without gore (no severed limbs, no pools of blood);
   cultures shown with respect and care, never as caricature.
6. **No real people, no existing film/game characters, no real flags or
   religious emblems used as logos.**
7. When the words and the reference images seem to disagree on style, **follow
   the reference images.**

## 3. Style bible

**Medium.** High-detail digital painting — "epic painterly fantasy". Visible,
controlled brushwork; crisp detail where the eye should go, softer atmospheric
detail elsewhere. **Not** a photograph, **not** a 3D render, **not** anime or
manga, **not** comic ink lines, **not** flat vector.

**Light.** Cinematic and warm-against-cool: golden-hour key light, cool blue
shadows, god-rays through clouds, mist and haze for depth, a bright rim light
that separates figures from backgrounds. Magic is an accent light: beacon fire,
glowing runes, sun discs, spirit lanterns.

**Colour.** Saturated but natural. Each pantheon has a palette (below); a
story's villain may darken or desaturate his own colours, but stays in his
pantheon's family.

| Pantheon | Main colours | Accents | Motifs to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greek | royal blue `#1F3F8F`, white marble `#ECE6DA` | gold `#C29A52`, bronze, olive green | laurel, owl, meander (key) border, lightning, the sea |
| Norse | night navy `#1B2A44`, steel silver `#C9D1DA` | fur browns, ember orange, aurora teal `#3FD6B0` | knotwork, ravens, wolves, carved rune-like marks, snow and ice |
| Egyptian | sand gold `#D6A842`, white linen `#F1EBDD` | lapis blue `#1F4FA0`, carnelian red `#A3262A`, turquoise | sun disc, spread wings, lotus, papyrus, obelisks |
| Japanese | vermilion `#C0262D`, black lacquer `#1A1414`, white `#F2EDE4` | cherry-blossom pink `#F2A7C3`, gold | torii gates, paper and stone lanterns, cherry blossom, waves, clouds |
| Celtic | forest green `#2F5D34`, gold `#C9A24A` | grey stone, woad blue, heather purple | spirals and triskeles, knotwork, standing stones, stags, mist |
| Mesoamerican | turquoise `#1AA39A`, crimson `#B3262B` | jade green, gold `#D2A440`, quetzal-green feathers | feathered serpents, step-fret patterns, stepped pyramids, sun stones |

**Faces and bodies.** Realistic adult proportions; expressive, grounded faces
like the hero cards on the pantheon boards (R7); believable skin with a
painterly finish; features that fit each culture.

**Costume.** Mythic-historical, ornate but readable at small size; gold trim
and the pantheon's colours; no modern objects.

**The game's frame colours** are deep navy and burnished gold. Images do not
need to contain them, but should sit comfortably inside a navy-and-gold frame.

## 5. Group A — Dialogue portraits (35)

### 5.0 Rules for all 35 portraits

* **Size:** 1024 × 1536 px, PNG with a **transparent background** (alpha).
  If a transparent background is impossible, use a perfectly flat mid-grey
  `#7F7F7F` with no gradient, no shadow and no floor, so it can be cut out.
* **Framing:** waist-up. The top of the head (or helmet, crown, headdress)
  about 6–10 % below the top edge; the eyes about 22–26 % from the top; the
  figure cut at the hips by the bottom edge. Shoulders fill about 70 % of the
  width. Large headdresses, wings or horns may reach the top edge but are not
  cut off by the sides.
* **Pose:** body turned three-quarters toward the **right side of the image**,
  head turned a little more toward the viewer, eyes on the viewer unless the
  description says otherwise. Hands visible when they hold the character's
  prop. Nothing asymmetric that must read correctly when mirrored (no writing,
  no one-sided emblem that matters).
* **Light (identical for all 35):** warm key light from the upper left, a cool
  rim light on the right edge of the figure, soft fill. The same light lets
  any two portraits face each other in one scene.
* **Finish:** the look of the hero and god cards on the pantheon boards (R7):
  painterly, detailed costume, a clear silhouette that reads at 160 px tall.
* **Age and build** as written. Expressions as written — these are the
  character's default mood for every line they speak.
