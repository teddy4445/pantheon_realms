# Research and inspiration
Research reviewed 2026-10-18. These are inspirations for original game content, not assertions that the invented equipment names or gameplay powers occur in ancient texts. No source artwork, prose or Heroes data table was copied into the art pack.

## Heroes mechanics adapted
- **Heroes III:** temporary battlefield obstacles and expendable copies inspired Glass Bulwark and Xolotl's Twin. Our versions have explicit anti-sealing, no-ability and no-victory-body restrictions. Reference: [original Restoration of Erathia manual, official Steam distribution](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/297000/manuals/BONUS_Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic_III_HDEdition_OldManual1999_EN.pdf?t=1699009789). The CDN PDF did not open successfully during this audit; indexed original-manual excerpts were available. Do not treat our numbers as Heroes III values.
- **Heroes IV:** displacement, ranged denial, enchantment removal and anti-summon magic suggested Tide Pearl Turn, Siren's Refrain, Unweave and Spellground. Reference: [original owner manual, mirrored transcription](https://manualzz.com/doc/54094714/games-pc-heroes-of-might-and-magic-iv-owner-manual).
- **Heroes V:** spell circles, guild-based learning and equipment decisions informed tier/access and loot organization. Reference: [official English manual](https://shared.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/15170/manuals/manual_english.pdf?t=1692823544).
- Adventure mobility is deliberately less permissive than unrestricted teleportation: this game's campaign boundaries, ships and exploration still matter. This is our design decision, not a claim about those games.

## Mythological motifs
| Family | Source motif used | New game adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Greek | Ariadne's guiding thread; Aeolus's contained winds | Route efficiency spool; limited maritime movement bag |
| Greek | Golden Fleece and bronze Talos | Shared army barrier mantle; first-arrow bronze protection |
| Norse | Gleipnir, Skidbladnir, Idunn and Mimir in Eddic tradition | Short root, boarding keel, bounded front healing, spell-cost cup |
| Egyptian | Protective knot/amulet traditions, sun and sacred animal motifs | Tyet ward, ibis writing tools, river and judgment magic |
| Japanese | Tide jewels, Uzume's performance, Hidesato's centipede hunt | One-hex push, cleanse, anti-large ranged artifact |
| Celtic | Nuada's silver arm and Dian Cecht's healing | Cleansing bracer and bounded front-creature healing needle |
| Mesoamerican | Ehecatl's wind/conch association and Xiuhcoatl's serpent form | Flying opener, maritime call and firebrand |
| General | Original fantasy survey, courier, dispel and protective motifs | Shared toolkit across all pantheons |

Mesoamerican content follows the game's existing combined pantheon; the named gods here are specifically rooted in Mexica/Nahua traditions. Celtic content draws on more than one Celtic literary tradition; it is not a claim of a single uniform historical pantheon.
