1. THE SHATTERED CROWN
ID: shattered_crown
Selection summary: Separate island kingdoms surround the broken Crown. Sail for distant riches, capture landing beaches, and carry your army across the sea to conquer.
Supported seats for first release: 2, 3 or 6. Every existing pantheon is selectable in every seat. Do not introduce new heroes or creatures.
Sizes: Huge for first release, radius25. Other sizes disabled until they pass the same capacity and naval tests.
Default victory: hold_relic, 7 consecutive owner dawns using existing hold-site semantics and existing rival-elimination alternative. Elimination-only is also supported.
Water target: 70–77% of non-void terrain. Reference:73.50%.
Topology: six outer home-size islands, six small inner resource islands, one isolated central objective island. Water completely separates all thirteen.
Pacing:
- Opening: secure your own island's wood, stone and supplies. Your empty ship is accessible immediately, with no guard or payment required.
- Expansion: choose an inner resource island or a clockwise/counterclockwise raid. Resources and neutral recruitment are distributed; the center is not the only worthwhile destination.
- Conflict: conquer landing beaches and sail around a threatened route. Every capital has at least two separated viable landings.
- Endgame: sail to and defend the central relic, or invade every rival island. No continent-wide land corridor ever appears.
Seat assignment on the reference six-island ring: 6 uses slots0–5; 3 uses0,2,4; 2 uses0,3. Unused slots contain identically budgeted neutral opportunities, no player town, no free hero or starting ship. Two/three-player arrangements must remain symmetric under the relevant seat rotation. Randomize seat-to-island assignment deterministically.
Central island: four separated legal landing clusters, an ordinary land path from each to the shrine and enough unoccupied space for disembarking. Existing shrine art and guardian creature pools. In elimination-only mode use the existing guarded central gold mine instead.
Inner resource islands: mirror reward/guard budgets, not a mandatory ordered quest chain. One rare-resource mine or equal-value treasure site per island; optional habitat-valid neutral recruitment from existing content.
Reference geometry: design/shattered_crown.topology.json and design/shattered_crown.layout.svg. Coordinates are centered axial; never confuse with tactical odd-r cells.

2. THE SERPENT STRAITS
ID: serpent_straits
Selection summary: Rival harbors face a maze of island straits. Seize the inner passages or sail around the outer coast to land behind the enemy.
Supported seats for first release: 2, human/AI or hot-seat, any pair of existing pantheons. This is deliberately a naval duel; do not advertise four/six-player support.
Sizes: Huge, radius25 for first release.
Default victory: elimination. Optional existing hold_relic with7 consecutive owner dawns on the isolated central island.
Water target: 66–74%. Reference:69.81%.
Topology: two large disconnected home islands, two long curved neutral islands separating inner and outer sailing routes, and one isolated central objective island.
Pacing:
- Opening: build your army on the home island and choose a clockwise or counterclockwise departure.
- Expansion: land on either curved island for extra income, a watchtower or habitat-valid recruits. These are optional staging areas, not required keys.
- Conflict: short inner passages make interception attractive; the longer outer sea stays open for flanking. Neither a single dock occupant nor a single ship can seal every route.
- Endgame: invade from the forward beach, rear cove or side beach and attack the opposing coastal capital on land. There is no compulsory left-to-right sequence.
At least three landing clusters per home island, two per curved island, three on the center. Inner shipping corridors at least2 water hexes wide; outer bypass at least3 except at actual coastline endpoints. Validate these after object placement.
Gold/wood values and guardian budgets mirror180degrees between the two empires. Coastal forest/rock dressing varies visually without changing movement or reward budgets.
Reference geometry: design/serpent_straits.topology.json and design/serpent_straits.layout.svg. The conceptual painting includes extra distant scenery; it is not an exact tactical chart.

3. MAKING SHIPS NECESSARY, NOT JUST OPTIONAL
Use normal sea movement and boarding rules from the naval kit. No water damage, weather simulation, sea monsters, cannons, new naval unit roster or additional currencies are required.
- Every opposing capital is in another land-connected component.
- The relic/central gold mine is also in a separate land component.
- Disable portals for these templates, visibly locked in setup with reason “Ships connect these islands.” Force portals:false in validation as well; do not silently accept a persisted true value.
- No sandbars, bridges, ferries, scripted relocation, sluices draining inter-island channels or portal quest rewards. No road repair may replace sea with land.
- Audit adventure spells and movement powers. A hero may not cross water, target an undiscovered foreign island, teleport into an unvisited town across the sea or use a flying army to bypass embarking. Enable a scenario-level naval-only inter-island traversal rule rather than globally deleting spells. A legal recall to a previously visited friendly town can remain only if it cannot provide first access to a new island; document and test this carefully.
- Allied units and AI must obey exactly the same traversal rules.
- Elimination can still occur by an invading rival losing on your own shore; do not add an artificial “you must personally sail once” victory counter. The requirement is structural: no land-only way to reach and conquer rival holdings or claim the central victory site.
- Revealed map changes information, never passability. Land caravans cannot cross these seas in this release.
- A stationary ship can be attacked by a hostile occupied ship using the two-deck bridge battle. Troops must disembark before attacking a shore army/town; no ship-to-shore cannon system.

4. STARTS, COASTS AND NO-SOFTLOCK RULES
Starting hero, faction, army, wallet and town content follow current setup rules. Add one empty owner-associated ship per active player as a scenario grant, independent of StartWealth and never sold for gold.
Place it adjacent to a legal reachable embark beach near the starting town, in the connected sea. No guard on the approach. Ship path must join both available outbound sea routes.
Use the existing naval-kit ship purchase:1500Gold+10Wood at an owned coastal town, immediate placement in a free legal launch-water cell. No new shipyard building. Validate the actual town footprint has adjacent water and its normal gate remains land-reachable. The reference anchors are guides; adjust footprint-safe positions symmetrically in production.
Each home island guarantees an accessible sawmill, quarry, early supplies, renewable town income, normal growth and a safe land path to embark. Replacement-ship wood comes from guaranteed renewable wood income, not a one-time chest. Preserve normal recruitment budgets.
Give each owned coastal town at least two legal launch cells. If both occupied, show a reason; do not spawn a ship under another ship or delete blockers.
At least two separated beach approaches per home island. A single opposing unit may occupy one beach but cannot permanently make the island unreachable while another beach is clear.
Disembarking consumes remaining movement according to the naval rules; route estimates must include that. Save final destination across days and pause for transition confirmations or battles.
Empty abandoned ships must not create unremovable naval walls: compatible boarding/ownership interaction or a legal alternative route is required. No free destroy-own-ship refunds.
Optional unowned towns obey the normal none/own/other/both setting. Place equivalent budgets on remote islands; enlarge matching islands symmetrically if required. Fail validation if footprints, gates, sites and landing capacity cannot fit. Do not hide missing towns or duplicate them in both home and remote regions.

5. RANDOMIZATION AND FAIRNESS
These are seeded templates, not two fixed scenarios. Use the exact reference graphs as safe starting skeletons.
Keep coast safety bands, capital zones, legal beaches, launch cells and sea corridors reserved. Apply bounded coast jitter to unreserved edges only, in the same rotation/reflection orbit. Revalidate all land and water connectivity after each candidate. Apply themed land tiles/decor, site variants, reward identities and guardian choices from catalog pools with equal budgets per player.
Do not scale this dense topology blindly down to Small or Medium.
Use independent RNG streams for terrain, content and decoration. Generator identity e.g. pr-naval-templates-1; include template, version, seed and all relevant settings in map hash/save. Leave legacy map seeds and SKIRMISH_NOISE_LABEL unchanged.
Try at most32 deterministic candidate variants; if invalid, use the reference-shaped topology and rerun complete content placement checks. If those fail, return an explicit generation error; never silently carve a causeway, grant a teleport or return an invalid map.
The stored graphs are baseline geometry only. Their sea connectivity and separated land components were tested, but the production randomized variants, footprints, AI and rules have not been implemented here.
For symmetry, compare actual movement cost from gate to beach and first neutral opportunity; maximum difference1 hex at equal terrain. Match home usable land, resource/guardian budgets and accessible shoreline count. Random decorative trees must not add invisible collision.
