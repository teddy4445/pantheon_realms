Keep both armies able to fight

Hippocampi fly. The other four families use repeatable ranged attacks from the water. Proposed deck harpoons give even a melee-only ship army a weak way to fight back, consuming that stack's action. The railing blocks walking, not projectiles.

Base → upgraded

Ten native 1254 × 1254 portraits. These are opaque UI portraits, not transparent full-body sprites or animations. Combat abilities and upgrade names are game adaptations.
