COASTAL WATCHTOWERS
Suggested new kind coastal_watchtower (or an explicit mode on a versioned watchtower definition), radius6. Existing watchtower currently only reveals configured site IDs on first visit: packages/rules/src/systems/adventure.ts case watchtower. Do not globally turn old campaign towers into capturable surveillance towers.
Placement: valid coastal land hex, adjacent to navigable water, legal land approach and a nearby disembark beach. Never place on a water tile or over a required channel. Use warm art for sand/temperate coasts, cold art for rock/cool/marsh-adjacent coasts. Neutral across all six pantheons.
Visit/capture: a land-mode hero reaches the actual tower interaction cell; an embarked hero must first disembark legally. Optional existing neutral guards can protect it. No new remote ship capture. On successful capture set ownerId, emit one capture event and recompute vision.
Benefit: owner receives live ordinary visibility within6 adventure hexes while owning it, including sea lanes. Newly seen terrain remains explored after capture loss, but live hostile positions stop updating. A tower reports no exact enemy troop counts, artifacts, mana or invisible units. Pass observations through the current/scouting-kit information policy. Passive tower observation is an ordinary distant sighting; it does not inherit a remote hero's Scouting skill.
Alert: “Enemy ship sighted near [tower]” only when a genuinely visible hostile ship enters the tower's observation range. Coalesce overlap from several towers, at most one alert per ship per owner-turn. Do not reveal a ship through fog or replay historical alerts on load.
States: neutral -> owned -> recaptured. Neutral flag omitted; owned flag uses actual player color. Tower remains static, no building bob or flame animation on an unlit brazier required. Guarded/visited badges use existing controls.
Save owner, site state and any event dedup keys. Restore shared/allied vision according to existing alliance rules; do not invent a new alliance system.
A radius6 tower is a proposed starting value. Balance through placements; avoid exposing a rival's starting harbor on day1 by default.

FLOATING WOOD / STONE / GOLD
Display names: Driftwood Bundle, Stone Cargo, Lost Treasury.
Use existing resource types wood, stone, gold and the existing pickup/grantReward transaction where practical. Site definition explicitly requires naval interaction; do not globally make ordinary land pickups reachable from water.
Single water hex, stationary logical coordinate. A subtle visual bob0.8–1.5px/3–4seconds is enough; no logical current/drift simulation. Disable bob under reduced motion.
The hero must be embarked in an occupied ship, and normally moves onto the pickup hex. Pay normal water movement cost. Pickups do not block movement or become enemy ships. Resolve site action after movement. A land hero standing next to it cannot collect remotely. Flying army composition gives no exception.
Proposed reward per pickup: wood3–6, stone3–6, gold250–500. Sample once from map seed/content RNG at generation, store the chosen reward, never reroll on hover or reload. Equal seed/symmetric region budget means equal rewards even when visual arrangement varies.
Atomic collection: verify active hero/ownership/legal movement and unclaimed site; mark claimed and grant reward once in one command. Repeated commands/load cannot duplicate income. Show existing resource toast and remove/fade sprite over250ms. No confirmation modal for routine pickups. Keep a claimed tombstone/state for persistence even if renderer hides it.
Do not award the wooden raft as extra wood when collecting stone/gold.
Stone and gold sit on barrel-supported cargo rafts, so they visually float; these are one-time salvage, not resource mines or daily production.
Never fill a launch cell, required landing cell, occupied ship cell or wreck interaction ring with forced collectibles. Pickups themselves remain traversable, but reserve important UI/visual lanes.
Map suggestions:1–2 pickups of each resource per active-player region in new Huge naval templates, with matching budgets and2+hex visual spacing. Additional shared treasures can appear farther offshore. Not a weekly respawn system.

SHIPWRECK EXPEDITIONS
Two interchangeable map skins: broken mast with torn sail, and split exposed hull. Names/guard/reward are seeded site data, never guessed from the image.
Placement: a stationary water landmark occupying one logical hex, with at least two free adjacent water approach cells in a connected sea. Wreck occupies its own anchor, so route to a legal adjacent approach rather than moving the ship into its hull. Keep wrecks away from launch cells and do not cut the last navigable route. Decorative sprite spill is non-colliding.
Interaction: only an adjacent hero in a ship may inspect/explore. “Explore” costs100 movement, requires at least100 available, and starts a persisted encounter; “Leave” costs0. Moving there already paid normal sailing cost. User must confirm a guarded fight; never autoplay it by clicking a route.
Panel uses shipwreck-expedition-scene.png, name, scouting-allowed danger information, known/unknown reward indication, Explore and Leave controls. Paint does not reveal which guards are aboard.
Proposed outcome table, sampled once at generation:
-50% abandoned cargo: reward500Gold +4Wood, no fight.
-40% guarded salvage: reward1000Gold +8Wood after victory; existing catalog Brigand and Brigand Cutthroat are suitable humanoid guards. Example14brigands+4cutthroats is only a starting point; scale through current guard-budget rules, no new pirate art.
-10% guarded rare cache:750Gold plus one existing eligible minor artifact; stronger existing guards using the same budget system. Select and persist artifactId at generation. Do not introduce a new artifact.
Weighting/rewards are proposed. Any generated guard must use valid current catalog IDs, normal stack limits and a legal full deployment footprint. Do not spawn the five proposed sea creatures from this document.
Combat: use prior two-deck boarding battlefield, explained as raiders moored alongside the wreck. This is an ordinary land-on-deck fight with existing ground rules; no aquatic creatures are required. Caller retains the visiting hero's ship on its approach hex. The wreck is a site, NOT a hostile hero ship that should be deleted by ship-vs-ship resolution.
Add a distinct wreck encounter origin/context. On victory, clear the site, grant rewards once, return hero to the same ship. On defeat, use hero/army defeat rules and the naval ship-loss contract; no duplicate hero or orphan ship. Surviving neutral guard state follows existing encounter persistence consistently. Retreat/cancel rules must not teleport a hero onto land.
If artifact inventory cannot accept the reward, preserve a pending artifact reward attached to the cleared site/player and show the ordinary swap/leave choice. Already-granted resources cannot be granted again when claiming the artifact. Do not discard the artifact silently.
State: untouched, encounterPending, cleared, pendingReward if necessary. Persist guard definition, reward, battle reference and claim state. Retry commands must be idempotent. A completed site can fade from the map or remain non-interactive with a cleared overlay; no new wreck image is needed.
Campaign combat remains manual where campaign rules prohibit automatic resolution. These additions do not change that policy.

FIVE MYTH-ROOTED NEUTRAL SEA CREATURES — REVISED PROPOSALS
Supersedes the generic wildlife suggestions. Neutral describes gameplay allegiance; all creatures must be rooted in traditions represented by the game. No creature visuals are generated yet. Sources support myth identity; roles, sizes and abilities below are proposed game adaptations.

1. Hippocampus
Myth root: Greek myth; fish-tailed horse associated with sea gods.
Gameplay: Proposed fast sea scout and flanking unit.
Compatibility: Aquatic; water combat required.
Source: https://www.theoi.com/Ther/Hippokampoi.html

2. Cetus (Ketos)
Myth root: Greek myth; sea monster in the Perseus and Andromeda story.
Gameplay: Proposed heavy coastal guardian with a sweeping bite.
Compatibility: Aquatic; scale and abilities are game adaptations.
Source: https://www.theoi.com/Ther/KetosAithiopios.html

3. Hafgufa
Myth root: Medieval Norse maritime tradition; enormous sea creature described in the King's Mirror.
Gameplay: Proposed rare sea boss guarding a rich wreck.
Compatibility: Aquatic; boss rather than ordinary recruit.
Source: https://www.abc.net.au/news/science/2023-03-01/ancient-texts-norse-saga-hafgufa-whale-trap-feeding/102027600

4. Isonade
Myth root: Japanese folklore; sea monster with a hooked or barbed tail.
Gameplay: Proposed ambusher whose tail sweep briefly reduces movement.
Compatibility: Aquatic; no instant drowning or forced movement into illegal cells.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isonade

5. Abtu and Ant (Anet)
Myth root: Egyptian sacred fish associated with the solar boat in Book of the Dead translations.
Gameplay: Proposed paired support encounter granting a temporary scouting blessing.
Compatibility: Myth-rooted sea-map adaptation, not traditionally a naval combat unit.
Source: https://www.holybooks.com/wp-content/uploads/Egyptian-Book-of-the-Dead.pdf

Keep Abtu/Ant as a paired sacred-fish encounter, not a claim that Egyptian texts describe recruitable war-fish. Hafgufa belongs to medieval Norse sea tradition. These five candidates cover four represented traditions; no claim of one creature per pantheon. None should be placed on the existing dry boarding deck without an explicit aquatic combat design. Existing faction kelpies/selkies are not new neutral creatures. Do not implement this proposed roster until approved.
