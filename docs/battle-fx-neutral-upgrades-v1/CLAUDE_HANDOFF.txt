5. FX ATLAS AND TIMING CONTRACT
-------------------------------
Every FX sheet is 4 columns × 4 rows. Read projectile-profiles.json for EXACT native rectangles, anchors and per-unit mappings. Do not assume a 1024 atlas, square canvas, integer width/4, or identical dimensions across files. The atlatl and sling sheets have different native dimensions from the others.

Standard row order, zero-based:
0 = base travel, four painted poses.
1 = base impact, four sequential expansion/dissipation poses.
2 = upgraded travel, four painted poses.
3 = upgraded impact, four sequential expansion/dissipation poses.
Siege row 0/1 = turret arrow travel/impact; row 2/3 = catapult stone travel/impact.

Travel art faces +X (right). Rotate along the trajectory tangent when shooting left or diagonally. Do not mirror a tangent-rotated projectile twice. Keep impact texture upright unless a deliberate minor seeded rotation is appropriate for dust; do not spin spectral/fire direction arbitrarily.

Physical arrows, javelins, darts, stones and sling ammunition: use travel frame 0 with world motion by default. The additional poses are optional variants, not required spinning animation. This prevents shape/centre wobble. Magical bolts, breath, wails and flame can loop their four travel frames at 10 fps.

Suggested paths:
• Heavy boulders/granite/siege stones: high ballistic arc, 320–650 ms travel.
• Javelins/darts/arrows/slings: shallow ballistic arc, 220–500 ms.
• Arcane, frost breath, holy flame, spectral wail, serpent fire: direct path, 220–500 ms.
Scale duration with range and battle speed, clamped to the profile's range. The standalone preview uses a slower fixed 650 ms flight for inspection; that is not the gameplay requirement.

Release at the attack/shoot animation's release moment. Use a per-unit muzzle/hand/mouth socket relative to its sprite anchor and facing, not one chest origin for every creature. The profile lists the logical source point; exact pixel offsets require calibration in the live renderer. Keep two-hex units' contact and source points correct when mirrored.

On arrival: remove travel sprite, start the profile's impact once at the actual contact point, trigger existing hit reaction and damage-text presentation together. Impact is 4 frames at 12 fps (10 fps for heavy stone); fade alpha during its final 100 ms to avoid a hard pop. Never loop an impact. Reduced-effects mode may show the first impact frame with a short fade.

Use normal premultiplied-alpha compositing initially. Do not treat invisible RGB around transparent pixels as visible background and do not alpha-key black. Some wisps retain very low alpha at grid edges; normal alpha is intentional. Avoid additive blending for opaque physical dust and fragments. A restrained additive core could later be layered on magic only, after checking light/snow maps.

The initial sizeHex/impactSizeHex values refer to the full source cell, including transparent margins. Do not also normalize every visible bbox to that size or the FX will become huge. Creature frame sourceScale and constant set scale must likewise be preserved.

No new damage, splash radius, freeze/slow, burning ground, fear, poison, chain lightning or debuffs. All post-hit particles are cosmetic. Existing damage/resistance rules remain authoritative. Never emit particles per soldier in a stack. Use one capped effect per actual shot event; existing multi-shot attacks can play their actual separate shots without changing hit count.
