Pantheon Realms - campaign and battle magic update, 22 October 2026
Version remains 1.0.0.

Boarding and landing cost one ordinary movement step. Route arrival estimates,
confirmations and movement messages use the same rule. Safe Landing never makes
landing worse than an ordinary step.

All v2 regional maps gain up to six reachable optional guarded prospects/supply
caches, placed away from narrow passages and authored roads. The Confluence's
12 regional chapters gain ten optional guarded locations each, stronger story
and passage guards, more distant investigations, required Civic Hall II and a
creature dwelling, and two announced enemy patrols. Clearing their home outposts
stops them. Enemy forces muster after twelve days. Coastal boarding approaches
are reserved so destination-only sites cannot obstruct the hero.

Surviving AI/neutral armies grow 12% at each new week, minimum one creature per
stack. This includes guards, hostile heroes and unowned/AI garrisons, not human
armies. Cleared sites never respawn. Growth is applied once per week.

Claim objectives explicitly say to open the Journal. New Game's chronicle reads
saved campaign progress and offers replay of completed chapters and continuation
to available chapters. Completed chapters receive a dedicated campaign save.
Replay uses the existing protected practice branch; it preserves the main run.

The map's visible/explored mask advances with each walked or sailed hex instead
of revealing the committed destination immediately. Camera follows the avatar.

Attack miracles now support percentage-of-current-HP damage with a minimum
flat amount. Existing Wrath/Zeus miracles use 12%/18%; the new miracles use 16%
and 24%. AI evaluation uses the same scaling. Weekly Favor limits still apply.

42 new attack spells: six for each pantheon and six shared; two per tier 1-3
in each group. Each tier includes a direct and an area attack. 12 new native
attack miracles: two per pantheon. All use native SVG icons and procedural
colour, travel, impact and sigil animations. No large sprite strips added.
Dev gallery: apps/client/magic-preview.html (run the development server).

New campaign layouts require a new or replayed chapter; existing saves keep
current map data. Numerical difficulty is a first balancing pass and needs
human playtesting across full campaigns.
