01. IMPLEMENTATION REQUEST

Implement city dedications for all six pantheons using existing gods, creatures, buildings and art. Each active dedication grants exactly three local benefits: one weekly resource gift, extra production of one favored creature family, and one town-service advantage. There are 60 authored profiles: all 6 patrons and all 54 current minor gods. This package is a design and art-reuse handoff based on game version 1.4.0, inspected 17 October 2026. It does not modify or implement the game. All numerical values are proposed starting balance, not playtest results. Use the delivered explicit profiles; do not infer benefits by parsing god names/domains at runtime. Scope: resources, creature recruitment stock and existing town services. Do NOT add a new paid weekly ritual menu, combat stances, new gods, new creatures, new buildings, free troops, new spell effects or faction-wide combat buffs. Existing divine commitments, hero gifts, spell eligibility, pantheon signatures and campaign story gates remain in place except the explicit direct-god economic replacement below. The brief refinements supersede the earlier illustrative chat table: Anubis gives 1 Crystal, Inari 600 Gold, and Tlaloc favors Cenote Crocodiles rather than all tier 1–3 dwellings. The attached 60-row JSON is the single authoritative proposal for this delivery.

03. PLAYER RULES AND ELIGIBILITY

A city can have ONE active dedication and at most ONE pending replacement. Owning several cities permits different dedications. A god may be honored in several cities; each eligible city earns its own local benefits. No empire-wide multiplier or capital-only restriction. Eligibility: the scenario enables cityDedicationsV1; the active player owns the city; the city’s pantheon matches the player and god; the city has Temple level II; the god is either the player’s CURRENT starting patron or an already committed minor god. Honor the existing mission god allowlist and commitment exclusivity. Dedication does not make a new commitment, bypass a choice group, or charge the original Favor cost again. The six patrons are included as useful starter options once Temple II exists. Foreign-pantheon conquered cities retain ordinary buildings, economy and recruits, but cannot receive a new dedication from this player’s incompatible pantheon. Explain this in the interface. Do not silently convert the town or invent foreign god commitments. FIRST DEDICATION: free, activated immediately for services. It grants no resources or population on selection. First recurring resource/growth reward is the next world-week boundary (normally days 8, 15, 22...). Creating a dedication before a rollover does not grant missed previous weeks. SWITCHING: costs 500 Gold; schedule the new god for the next world-week boundary. The current god remains active until then. Only one pending switch; reject a second request or selecting the active/pending god again. Show exact cost, current benefits, replacement benefits and activation day before confirmation. This version has no cancel/reorder action; say so in confirmation. The fee is spent on confirmation and is not refunded if the city is captured before activation. Temple loss/invalid eligibility: active dedication becomes dormant immediately; no services, weekly gift or growth credit while invalid. Keep its identity/credits for the same owner. A pending switch is evaluated at its scheduled boundary; if it is temporarily invalid, keep it pending and try at the next weekly boundary, with the reason displayed. It produces no replacement benefits while waiting. The currently active god continues only if independently eligible. CAPTURE/OWNER CHANGE: clear active god, pending switch and all growth credits immediately, including on loss to a neutral owner. Keep the historical per-town payout-week stamp. The conqueror receives no inherited gift/service and may make a fresh eligible dedication. Recapture does not restore the previous dedication. No capture, liberation, load, menu opening or god-selection event may trigger recurring payment. Ownership/script changes must all route through the clearing hook. A pending switch stores its requesting owner and may never activate for another player. Guard against stale UI commands by requoting on commit.

04. WEEKLY RESOURCES AND CREATURE PRODUCTION

RESOURCE: add the profile’s fixed resource amount to the owning player’s wallet at a world-week boundary, once per eligible city. No manual claim button, no free starting grant, no banked gifts for dormant weeks. Apply the existing owner incomePercent handicap exactly once, flooring each resource; preview the resulting amount. Do not include the gift in daily income or divide it by seven. CREATURE: extra stock belongs only to the favored family’s existing shared base/upgraded town population. The matching base dwelling and all normal family/covenant access must exist. No new creature family is unlocked; an upgraded dwelling is not required for the growth benefit. Upgrading the dwelling does not double the pool. Players still pay to recruit the units. Neutral hiring sites and neutral weekly stocks are unaffected. Production rates by favored family tier: • Tier 1–2: +2 creatures each eligible week. • Tier 3–5: +1 each eligible week. • Tier 6: +1 every two eligible weeks. • Tier 7: +1 every four eligible weeks. Use integer quarter credits to avoid rounding rare-unit growth down forever: profile weeklyGrowthQuarters is 8, 4, 2 or 1. At one eligible weekly update: credit = storedCredit[familyId] + weeklyGrowthQuarters extra = floor(credit / 4) storedCredit[familyId] = credit % 4 sharedPopulation[familyId] += extra Credits accrue only while the dedication AND matching built dwelling are eligible. Building the dwelling grants only its existing initial population, not divine stock or retroactive credits. Do not multiply this bonus by Citadel/Castle growth, weekly events or other growth modifiers. Calculate normal growth with its normal modifiers, then add this dedication bonus once. Keep retained non-god growth effects intact. Credit is stored per city and family, not globally. Switching to a god favoring the same family continues its credit. Switching to another family parks the old family’s remainder; only the currently favored family accrues. There is at most one favored-family credit update per town per week. Capture clears remainders. Temporary dormancy pauses them without catch-up. Player text for rare growth must be understandable: “One extra Bennu every 2 eligible weeks — 1 week remaining.” Show “Build the Bennu dwelling to begin” when absent. Show separate weekly resource eligibility and creature-growth eligibility; a missing dwelling does not block the resource gift or an unrelated service. Dawn order: advance world day; announce the existing weekly event; on a new week activate due valid pending dedications; preserve the existing income process; pay new weekly city gifts; apply ordinary weekly growth plus the one dedication credit update; refresh shops; refresh movement/mana; complete ordinary turn bookkeeping. Services of an activated pending god apply to subsequent commands, not to any already committed transaction.

05. REBALANCE EXISTING GOD BONUSES WITHOUT DOUBLE COUNTING

Feature ON: suppress ONLY direct GodDefinition.benefits entries of type daily_income and weekly_growth_bonus for ALL committed gods, whether or not a city currently honors them. Their new economic benefit comes through the local dedication profiles. Keep all their other benefits (combat stats, movement, recovery, morale, spell eligibility, offering rules, vision, etc.). There are 12 gods with affected direct economic entries; the exact removed and retained arrays are in legacy-benefit-replacement.json. Filter inside godEffects(), where source provenance is known. Do not filter the combined playerEffects() array by type: that would wrongly remove income or growth from signatures, weekly events and other sources. Do not mutate the immutable global catalog or remove the original entries for legacy games. Hero level-10/15 god gifts remain unchanged, including any daily_income effect on an already chosen hero perk. Temple Favor income, ordinary building income, mines, hero artifacts/skills, weekly events, signatures and existing paid divine-gift commands are unrelated and remain unchanged. Do not reuse TownState.lastGiftWeek (the paid gift system) as the dedication payout marker. This deliberately moves economic gods from passive empire-wide bonuses to city specialization. It is a material rebalance, especially Hermes, Njord, Hathor and Inari. Show the new effective empire blessing plus city options on god cards; do not leave old daily-income/growth benefitSummary text visible in new-mode games. Legacy-mode cards retain the old text. No guarantee is made that the proposed small weekly gifts replace the exact old economic power. Benchmark one-, three- and six-city empires and both short/long scenarios before final tuning. Change numbers in data rather than layering the old bonuses back in. Record any tuning and update visible summaries from the same rules data.

06. SERVICE RULES AND PRICE CALCULATION

TRAINING — Hero training 20% less Gold for weekly hero training; XP and the once-per-hero-per-week limit stay unchanged. TRADE — Market purchases 10% less Gold when buying Wood, Stone or Crystal at this city; selling prices stay unchanged. FORTIFY — Fortification construction 10% less Gold to build or upgrade this city’s Fort, Citadel and Castle; material costs and one build per day stay unchanged. LIBRARY — Library construction 10% less Gold to build or upgrade this city’s spell library; material costs and spell prerequisites stay unchanged. SPELLBOOK — Spellbook purchase 25% less Gold for a visiting hero’s spellbook. Learning eligible spells remains free, as in the current game. RECRUIT — Favored recruitment 5% less Gold per recruited member of the favored family, both unlocked forms. Other resources, population and access checks stay unchanged. UPGRADE — Favored troop training 15% less Gold on the normal per-unit upgrade price difference for the favored family in this city. No extra stock is consumed. EQUIPMENT — Artifact purchases 10% less Gold for purchases from this city’s Artifact Merchant or Workshop. Stocks, rarities and other resource costs stay unchanged. WATCH — Watchtower vision +2 hexes to this city’s existing vision radius while its Watchtower is built; no hero vision bonus and no fog-map reveal on dedication. DWELLING — Favored dwelling construction 10% less Gold to build or upgrade the favored family’s dwelling in this city. No new creature access or covenant is granted. CIVIC — Civic construction 10% less Gold on this city’s civic-hall upgrades; level prerequisites and construction limits stay unchanged. All services are LOCAL to the active eligible city and require their normal service building or normal build prerequisites. A discount never grants a building effect before construction. For construction discounts, the target building need not already exist, but all ordinary requirements do. Use archetype slot roles and the delivered town-role-bindings.json, never English display-name comparisons. The favored dwelling is found by familyId/dwelling metadata. Gold discount arithmetic: discountedUnitGold = ceil(normalUnitGold × (100 - percent) / 100). A positive normal cost remains at least 1; a zero cost remains zero. Other resources are unchanged. For recruit and upgrade quantities compute the discounted PER-UNIT price, then multiply by quantity. This avoids order-splitting exploits. Single recruit, recruit all/max, batch recruit, AI quote, preview and commit must share the same resolver. Only the active city dedication’s relevant service applies. For other independent percentage discounts, use the highest applicable percent instead of adding or compounding percentages. Compute discounts after the existing non-discount base-cost modifiers, once. Keep cost breakdowns (normal, saving, final) in quote results; commit requotes against current wallet, owner, buildings and dedication. Market: determine the existing tier from actual owned markets. For each resource, discountedBuyRate = max(ceil(normalBuyRate × 0.90), maximumOrdinarySellRateForThatResourceAcrossMarketTiers + 1). Sell rates and market count stay unchanged. A discounted city is not an extra market. Test every pair of towns and supported resource for a profitable immediate buy/sell loop; none is allowed. Troop upgrades: start from the ordinary positive base→upgraded catalog resource difference; discount only its Gold component per unit. Do not derive it from another city’s discounted recruitment prices. Require normal upgraded dwelling/covenant/access, selected base stack and space. Population is not consumed by training, as in existing rules. Training: normal 500 XP and once-per-hero-per-week gate are unchanged; the ordinary 1000 Gold example becomes 800. Visit several cities must not permit repeated training. Spellbooks: discount purchase cost only. Spell learning is ALREADY FREE after an eligible visit with a book; never introduce a learning fee. The existing library already restores all mana at dawn for a visiting hero, so do not add a redundant mana-restoration perk. Equipment: both existing shop types get the same discount when present; do not generate extra stock, reroll stock on dedication, increase rarity, or bypass artifact legality. Watchtower: add exactly 2 to the ordinary town radius only when its normal building is present; use state-aware resolution wherever town fog/vision is calculated. No hero scouting bonus. Examples: recruitment 61 Gold at 5% off => 58 each, ten cost 580. Upgrade difference 17 Gold at 15% off => 15 each. Construction 2500 Gold/5 Stone at 10% off => 2250 Gold/5 Stone. Trade common buy rate 500 => 450; original sell rate 50 stays 50.
