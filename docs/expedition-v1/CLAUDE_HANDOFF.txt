04. BATTLEFIELD CREATURE INSPECTION
Open via right-click, keyboard Inspect action, or an explicit accessible info control. Escape closes an unpinned panel. Pin keeps the selected unit's card visible while the pointer moves. It must not issue movement or attack orders, advance the active turn, or block essential battlefield controls. Keep the selected creature and target footprint visible alongside the card; use an overlay on small supported viewports.

Show creature form/name, living count, portrait, effective Attack and Defense, damage range per creature, effective speed, ammunition remaining/max, and retaliations remaining. Use existing authoritative combat resolvers, including hero specialties, items, abilities and temporary modifiers. Never duplicate the damage formula in UI. Provide a base-versus-effective breakdown on hover/focus where useful.

Show front-creature HP separately from stack count and total HP. For a positive totalHp and effective max creature HP H, front HP = ((totalHp−1) modulo H)+1; destroyed stack is0. Account for abilities granting two or unlimited retaliations, and retaliation suppression; do not always display1/1. Nonshooters show ammunition as not applicable.

Active effects use current effect icons with label, remaining rounds or next-activation expiry, and source. BattleStatusState currently has sourceId and expiresAtRound plus optional expiresOnActivationOf. Resolve source honestly; if the existing sourceId only identifies the spell, add explicit sourceHeroId/sourceStackId when applying the status. Never infer a caster from flavor text or expose a hidden source. A focus/hover tooltip can highlight a visible source on the battlefield.

The concept Peltast numbers are illustrative current-state examples. In the actual game, read the selected stack. Accessibility: keyboard focus and tooltip equivalents, high-contrast selected border, non-color cues, no mandatory nested scrolling.

05. SCOUTING THAT REVEALS INFORMATION GRADUALLY
Implement a rules/view-model projection, not CSS hiding. UI, accessible labels, tooltips, inspector data and route summaries receive only currently permitted knowledge. Own/allied armies retain their existing full information.

Current Scouting adds +1/+2/+3 vision; retain this and add intelligence range. Proposed policy is explicit in design/scouting-policy.json:
• Visible enemy at distance beyond3: distant sighting, banner/known owner and one broad strength band only.
• Within3 visible hexes: identify creature types, but quantities remain unknown.
• Scouting rank1/2/3 within2/3/4 visible hexes respectively: approximate quantity ranges. Rank0 never gains detailed counts just by adjacency.
• Every stage still requires current visibility. Distance alone cannot reveal through fog.
• Use best eligible observing friendly hero; towns without a scouting hero grant normal sighting/identification but no detailed skill report.

Quantity ranges are1–4,5–9,10–19,20–39,40–79,80–159,160–319,320+. Group identical visible creature IDs before reporting; do not leak duplicate hidden army slots. Differing base/upgraded forms remain distinct once identified. Never show exact remaining HP, hidden artifacts, commander spells or raw army valuation through these reports.

Broad strength uses catalog Gold cost × living count, grouped into the delivered bands. This is a coarse army-size indicator, not a battle-win prediction; it ignores hero strength and tactical conditions. Do not label an unknown enemy safe. Values are proposed and must be balanced.

Store last observed position, day and permitted snapshot fields per observing player. Outside vision show LAST KNOWN plus its date; do not update counts, composition, owner, position, flags or destruction remotely. Re-entry updates according to current observation level. An older detailed report may be available separately but must not masquerade as a current detailed estimate when the new observer only has sighting knowledge. Remove stale markers only when their location is inspected or a permitted event reveals their fate. Do not send the full enemy state into the report component.

06. RECRUITMENT CARAVANS
Source is an owned town with normally available unlocked recruits. Destination is an owned town reachable by explored traversable LAND. This first version does not put recruitment caravans on ships. A future amphibious caravan system is out of scope.

Dispatch screen:
• Select destination, creature form and quantities. Show available shared family population and exact source-town prices.
• Reuse existing recruitment quotes, including local city-dedication discounts if present. Base/upgraded forms consume one shared pool. No duplicated stock, free upgrades, divine access bypass or separate50-creature limit.
• Charge normal recruits plus proposed flat150Gold transport once. Maximum7 distinct creature stacks; merge identical IDs.
• Show route, travel estimate, destination stack compatibility, and known risks. Revalidate owner, stock, price, route and funds atomically at dispatch.
• Remove stock and funds and create one shipment in one successful transaction. Failed commands change none.

Movement:
• Proposed600 movement points per owner-turn; existing road cost75 and terrain costs. First movement at the next start of that owner's turn, not immediately on dispatch.
• Stable shipment-ID order; persist lastMovedTurn and an interrupted-processing cursor so save/load and battle interruptions cannot repeat movement.
• One-hex local vision only; do not reveal distant route threats. Route planning uses explored terrain, and newly encountered blockers pause safely. Known route clear is not a guarantee about hidden enemies.
• Friendly heroes/caravans may share world tiles; provide an actor picker. Do not silently let an enemy pass through or attack the wrong actor.
• Save destination and remaining route between days. Player should not have to reselect it.

Interception:
An enemy hero may attack a caravan on a reachable tile through a validated command. Cargo becomes a hero-less defending army on the ordinary local battlefield. No extra invisible escorts, hero bonuses or caravan spellcasting. The owning human controls defense where the game's turn policy allows; campaign player battles stay manual. Attacker and shipment are locked during the encounter. Defending cargo cannot retreat as if it had a commander. Persist surviving cargo back to the shipment on defensive victory; destroy the shipment if all cargo dies. Do not convert defeated troops into the attacker's army or pay duplicate loot.

Arrival:
Merge surviving cargo into destination garrison exactly once, preserving separate creature IDs. Revalidate ownership and capacity on arrival. Deposit the whole shipment atomically; if it does not fit, use awaiting_garrison and retain all troops. Arrival notification says Added to garrison, with View garrison/Continue; no Claim button or second charge.

Captured destination, blocked route or changed ownership:
Hold at a legal location and notify. Permit reroute to another owned reachable town without charging the recruits again. Do not discard cargo, teleport it, or deliver to an enemy. Source capture after paid dispatch does not change the shipment owner. No new cancellation/refund system in this version; a lost cargo shipment is not refunded.

Example in references:12Hoplites×60 +8Peltasts×100 +2Griffins×240 =2000Gold; transport150; total2150. Day9 dispatch/Day12 arrival is illustrative; actual ETA comes from integer path costs and scheduled budgets, including terrain and gate positions.

07. HERO SHIPS AND ADVENTURE MOVEMENT
One ship carries one existing hero and their normal army; no new creature headcount capacity or extra army slots. Hide the mounted map avatar while embarked. Show the current hero's owner-colored square flag on the hull, the same color source as the current land banner. Hero portrait/name appears on hover/selection; optional small portrait medallion distinguishes same-owner heroes. Do not color by pantheon or invent a new hero identity. Empty ships have an owner/neutral indicator but no fictitious hero aboard.

Ships are distinct persisted world entities with a stable shipId, owner, coord, heroId or null, state and facing. A hero is either on land or aboard one ship; prohibit duplicate occupancy and conflicting coordinates. Validate invariants on save/load, defeat and scripted relocation.

Acquisition: editor/scenarios can place neutral or owned empty ships. Proposed optional launch service at an owned coastal town costs1500Gold+10Wood. A coastal town must have an actual adjacent eligible water hex; choose a free launch location. This adds a service, not a new buildable building. The hero can embark from a legal adjacent shore. Keep scenario naval enable/disable rules; do not rewrite inland campaign layouts automatically.

Embark/disembark:
Requires a legal adjacent landing/empty friendly-or-neutral ship and at least100 remaining movement. Consume ALL remaining movement for the day to avoid transport-switch refresh exploits. Boarding an owned occupied ship is not embarkation. Disembarking leaves the empty owned hull in its water tile. Reject mountains, blocked town walls/gates, occupied hostile shore and inaccessible coast. No swimming through intervening land. Require explicit confirmation when a planned mixed-mode route reaches an embark/disembark step.

Sailing:
Water-to-water edges cost100, no road bonus. Use the hero's existing daily movement budget; mode changes never restore it. Land mode cannot walk on water, naval mode cannot cross land. Plan and commit through the same mode-aware deterministic pathfinder. Cache keys must include mode/ship eligibility; do not reuse land-only passability caches accidentally. A land bridge over water and normal traversable roads require explicit map topology, not texture guesses.

Preserve the hero's final destination and remaining route between days. Continue action resumes validated travel. Stop for new blockers, changed ownership, missing ship, battle or landing confirmation; keep a clear reason. Never silently auto-confirm combat. Panning does not change the fixed map camera angle.

Occupied ship versus occupied hostile ship on adjacent water creates a boarding encounter. There are no ranged cannon duels, sea catapults or persistent hull-HP economy in this first implementation. Empty neutral ships can be claimed by valid embarkation, not fought as imaginary armies.

Defeat/victory:
Use existing hero/army casualty and defeat behavior. Attacker victory moves the attacker's own ship into the contested water hex; defender victory leaves defender's ship in place. The loser's hull is removed with a short decorative wreck/sink playback; no second hero or ghost occupancy remains. A surviving hero never changes ship identity as a side effect of artwork. Any permitted retreat must choose a deterministic legal vacant water destination for the same ship and preserve existing retreat casualties; disable it with a reason if none exists. No automatic land teleport.

09. SHIP AND WAGON ANIMATION DELIVERY
This package deliberately provides directional painted bodies plus runtime animations, NOT frame-by-frame ship raster strips or a 3D rig. The animation is visible in index.html and the implementation reference is runtime/ship-motion.js. Do not register a static PNG as an eight-frame strip.

Ship states: Idle, Sailing, Embark, Disembark, Boarding, Hit and Sinking. Idle has subtle vertical buoyancy/roll; sailing adds stronger motion, live flag flutter and a directional wake. Map translation follows committed waypoint playback separately. Embark/disembark crossfade the existing mounted avatar and ship. Hit is short recoil feedback; it does not introduce a new hull-damage rule. Sinking is a one-shot aftermath transition to the supplied wreck.

Three facing textures E/NE/SE plus horizontal mirrors cover W/NW/SW. Pick direction from projected travel delta, not raw screen-independent axial names. Never rotate the entire flat sprite to steer, since that would tilt the mast. Flag position follows the ship anchor; text, portrait and flag emblem are not mirrored. Runtime tint affects the separate flag only, never the entire wooden ship.

Default suggested display is about1.2 hex widths of visible hull, with a banner minimum size legible at map zoom. Use visible bounds from asset-manifest.json and register stable ground anchors. Idle/hit movement is presentation displacement only, never a change of world coordinates. Reduced motion disables bob/roll/wake/recoil while retaining readable state changes.

Wagon motion is a small map token using directional images, path tween and restrained cart bob. It is not an anatomical horse gait animation strip. For this map-scale treatment, no full horse rig is needed. Arrival can use the text-free painting and existing creature portraits.

Existing catapult throw is an8-frame256×256 strip at12fps. Use its provided metadata, current stone projectile and existing firing sound. Synchronize visible impact to the committed structure-damage event; do not apply gameplay damage twice from animation callbacks.

Proposed starting stats:120HP, Defense6, movement0, no retaliation. Enemy legal melee/ranged attacks and direct damaging spells can target it.
