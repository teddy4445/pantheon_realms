Concrete fixes

Gate faces the wrong direction

Replace the broad gatehouse with the new west-facing gate and align it with the road and vertical wall. Keep adjacent destroyed wall sections visually clear.

Start Battle button

The vertical icon/text stack extends below the painted face. Adjust the layout and hit area together.

Parchment label contrast

Difficulty uses pale gold on pale parchment. Use the parchment ink token in this context.

Battle loading readiness

Commands and targeting can appear before the units and battlefield textures. Show loading or readable placeholders until the critical art is ready.

Internal town caption

Remove the placeholder-art caption from the finished town.

Journal: loading fallback, not a broken finished layout

The initial capture exposes dark text on the dark scene. After explicitly decoding the existing artwork, the two-page book is correct. Preload the critical skin and provide a readable fallback.

Six native gate atlases · 18 states

Intact, damaged, breached from left to right. Checkerboard is this viewer only. Native PNG alpha is preserved. The manifest supplies frame rectangles and per-state anchors. Source prompts and provenance are in the source pack.
