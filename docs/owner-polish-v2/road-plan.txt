Road alignment review

The renderer chooses one independent painted tile per road hex, from 63 masks.
Direction masks and grid positions already agree mathematically. The visible
inconsistencies are inside the painted pieces: edge width, rut positions and
junction shapes vary. Enlarging or shifting entire tiles cannot fix all six
edges at once.

Recommended next pass:
1. Draw every connection along a shared centerline with identical edge ports,
   width and paired wheel ruts. Build junctions from the same connected graph.
2. Apply the current dirt/cobble/sand/snow texture to those continuous surfaces.
   Texture should decorate geometry, not determine where a road joins.
3. Blend materials over one tile at biome boundaries instead of switching sharply.
4. Validate all 63 masks against their neighbours and suppress tiny generated
   loops where they do not connect meaningful destinations.

This pass changes map scale and camera tracking, but leaves road artwork intact
pending that dedicated geometry/texture pass.
